My life’s been interesting lately, as a CS grad, I am constantly writing code and at the forefront of what I’m doing and dictating the direction and speed at which my projects go.

Reading scared me when someone else (say, an author of a textbook or a code snippet) was in the front seat, telling the tale, and all I got to do was watch them navigate and show their experiences. I was insecure and scared, wondering if I could ever do what they did.

But due to my latest internship, I had to do tons and tons of reading and researching in the areas of sheaf theory and topology. To a novice CS student like me with little to no math experience, this honestly felt like magic. But after reading for 3–4 weeks non-stop with very little programming, I have to say:

**Reading is a lot of fun. And so is writing your thoughts.**

Lessons learnt, will be doing this more and more!
