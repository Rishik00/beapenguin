I have a very bad habit. I search — a lot. Not through perplexity or any other AI browser, but with good old Google.

## A chaotic mind is shown by a chaotic browser

See, as a researcher it is my job to (0) Find a problem (1) find the right paper/tool that solves the said problem and (2) Solve it. But this is a very difficult and tricky process, because of the curiosity and excitement which steps (0) and (1) give you constantly, turning you into **a kid in a candy store,** a point where everything is amazing and everything can solve your problem.

And at times, the difference between curiosity and distraction is so thin that it’s hard to catch the signal from the noise.

If you’ve ever felt that you cannot close any one of the 100 chrome tabs and all or most of them are important and you feel like ripping your head off into a 100 different pieces (one for each tab), welcome aboard! I’ve been there, I AM THERE, AND I CANNOT COME OUT OF IT.

## Time is your best friend, and also your filter

Firstly, congratulations that you’ve identified something that needs a little controlling and fixing. Pat yourself on the back! This isn’t a terrible problem to have, I believe that it’s an awesome problem to have because it teaches you **how to filter what’s important.**

Our minds only remember things that have left an impression. I don’t remember what my mom made yesterday, but I certainly remember the one time my crush made lunch for me (I might’ve wayy too many romance mangas).

I do this:

1. I open a 100 browser tabs, fill them with whatever I find flashy.
2. I leave them be, for a couple of days (maybe 1–3).
3. I come back, check again. And I realised that I forgot half of what these papers are, so I delete.
4. Add the rest to [my list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19NIqsAnXbqksZgopBxfayP4FG4Nj7n6nfrwDtYkvRNA/edit?usp=sharing)
5. Repeat.

Why do I think this works? Same as above, initially I found something that was interesting about the paper — maybe a new technique or writing style or a flashy title (honestly, title’s are becoming more and more boring). But are they remember worthy? Not all of them, but my mind initially doesn’t know that.

So we just keep them as is, let chrome torture my laptop for a couple of days; and I revisit this one window of tabs I’ve had open for a while. But something’s different now, my mind has mostly forgotten what majority of these papers even were about. And hence, I just delete all the ones the current me doesn’t like.

And the best part? This applies to any kind of reading/searching. You find many things that might help/solve problems but keeping them aside for 1–2 days helps your mind actually think through the hype and help you decide better.

Thanks for reading, hope you liked it and hope you also let time do it’s thing on your chrome tabs (and my romance manga reading list, I’m fed up of summer festivals)!
