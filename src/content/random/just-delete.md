I really love podcasts, especially those about exceptional individuals. Although I am a researcher, I’m very interested in learning about businesses and how exceptional individuals solve problems.

I still remember saying that I admire Elon Musk a lot. I mean, who doesn’t? He’s one of the richest men in the world, has built a lot of successful companies, and worked 22 hours a day!? But after hearing more about him and the way he operates, my mind immediately went like:

**Yeah, I don’t even want to be that.**

But we can take a lot of lessons from his mindset. And I really like the following principles he constantly seems to have applied in the past:

1. Question everything, even the requirements laid out. Ask yourself: why do we need this?
2. Delete, delete, delete
3. A maniacal sense of urgency is how we operate.

I chuckled when I heard the second principle. Deleting is heavily underrated; we always admire complexity and adding more features, but the best products always appeal to a wider audience due to **simplicity.**

Exceptional people like Elon and even Steve Jobs (hope you know who he is) question everything, specifically the **need for something**. If they think it’s not needed, **they delete it.** Because they know that **SIMPLICITY SCALES FAR FASTER THAN COMPLEXITY.**

Love that idea, hope we can all do that!
